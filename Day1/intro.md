# GRC (Governance, Risk, Compliance) – Day 1 Guide

## 📌 Overview
Welcome to the first day of the **GRC (Governance, Risk, Compliance)** course.  
This class is designed for beginners who want to understand **how businesses protect themselves beyond technical security**.  

> "Technical tools block attacks. GRC protects businesses from shutting down."

---

## 🎯 Day-1 Objectives
By the end of this session, students will:

1. Understand what **GRC is**.
2. Learn the differences between **Governance, Risk, and Compliance**.
3. Understand **why organizations need GRC**.
4. Know **real-world examples of GRC**.
5. Learn the **basic GRC lifecycle**.
6. Get introduced to **frameworks, standards, and tools**.
7. Identify **GRC job roles and daily tasks**.
8. Complete simple **homework to reinforce learning**.

---

## 📝 Pre-requisites
Students should have:

1. Basic understanding of **IT systems and networking**.
2. Awareness of **security tools** like firewalls, antivirus, SIEM (nice-to-have, not mandatory).
3. Basic computer skills (Excel, browsing, documentation).
4. Curiosity to understand **business and security together**.

> **Note:** No programming knowledge is required for Day-1.

## 🧩 What is GRC?

**GRC = Governance + Risk + Compliance**

### Layman Example (Apartment)
| Term | Explanation |
|------|------------|
| Governance | Apartment rules (visitors, noise) |
| Risk | What can go wrong? (fire, theft) |
| Compliance | Following laws/certificates (fire safety, electricity) |

### Corporate Example
- **Governance** → Policies for password, data, cloud access
- **Risk** → Data leak, cyber attack, vendor failure
- **Compliance** → ISO 27001, SOC2, GDPR, HIPAA

> **GRC ensures business continuity, compliance, and reputation protection.**

---

## 🧠 Part 1: Governance
**Governance = How decisions are made & enforced.**

### Key Concepts
- Policies, standards, procedures
- Roles and responsibilities
- Alignment with business goals

### Real-World Examples
- Password policy (12 chars, MFA, rotate 90 days)
- Cloud access policy (who can access production)
- Vendor risk policy

### Governance Roles
- Board of Directors
- CISO
- Security Architects
- GRC Team

---

## 🔥 Part 2: Risk Management
**Risk = Likelihood × Impact**

### Layman Example
- Riding a bike → Risk: accident, Mitigation: helmet

### Corporate Example
- AWS S3 bucket → Risk: public access → Mitigation: encryption, access control

### Risk Lifecycle
1. Identify
2. Analyze
3. Evaluate
4. Treat
5. Monitor

### Risk Treatment Types
| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| Mitigate | Enable encryption |
| Accept | Low-impact risk |
| Transfer | Cyber insurance |
| Avoid | Stop storing PII in public bucket |

---

## 📚 Part 3: Compliance
**Compliance = Following laws, standards, or contracts.**

### Layman Example
- Traffic rules, income tax filing, office attendance policy

### Corporate Examples
- ISO 27001
- SOC 2
- GDPR
- HIPAA
- PCI-DSS

### Audit Evidence Examples
- MFA logs
- Access control records
- Risk register
- Vendor questionnaires

---

## 🔧 Part 4: GRC vs Technical Security
| GRC | Technical Security |
|-----|-----------------|
| Policies | Firewalls |
| Risk register | SIEM / IDS |
| Audits | WAF |
| Evidence | Security logs |

> **Remember:** GRC doesn’t block attacks; it prevents business loss and legal penalties.
